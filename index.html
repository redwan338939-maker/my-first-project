Day 1 - Family & People

1. I - আমি - I am Redwan.

2. You - তুমি - You are my friend.

3. He - সে (ছেলে) - He is a good boy.

4. She - সে (মেয়ে) - She is my sister.

5. We - আমরা - We are learning English.

6. They - তারা - They are playing.

7. Boy - ছেলে - The boy is happy.

8. Girl - মেয়ে - The girl is reading.

9. Man - লোক - The man is my father.

10. Woman - মহিলা - The woman is my mother.
